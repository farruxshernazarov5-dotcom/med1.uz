**CHAPTER 3**

What is a Cookie?
A cookie is a small text file stored by a website in the browser of the User's device. It allows the
site to recognize return visits, maintain sessions, and remember settings.

Type                                   Description

Session cookie                         Deleted when the browser is closed

Persistent cookie                      Stored until a specified period

First-party                            Set by the med1.uz domain

Third-party                            Set by partner services (analytics, payments)

**CHAPTER 5**

Categories of Cookies We Use

1) Strictly Necessary Cookies
Mandatory for the operation of the Platform: authorization sessions, security tokens (CSRF), load
balancing. They do not require consent, as the service cannot be provided without them.

2) Functional Cookies
Language choice, interface theme, last selected filters, font size.

3) Analytical Cookies
Number of page views, error statistics, usage paths — in aggregated form to improve the service.

4) Marketing Cookies
Measuring advertising effectiveness and limiting repetitive displays. Only with explicit consent.

Category                     Consent                 Standard Period         Example Purpose

Strictly Necessary           Not required            Session – 12 months     Login, security

Functional                   Required                6 – 12 months           Language, settings

Analytical                   Required                1 – 24 months           Statistics

Marketing                    Required                1 – 12 months           Ad measurement
