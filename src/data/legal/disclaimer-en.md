**SPECIAL WARNING REGARDING ARTIFICIAL**
**INTELLIGENCE MODULES**
AI modules on the Platform are based on statistical probability models; they are not certified as
medical devices and are not considered clinical diagnostic tools. AI results are intended as a
decision support tool for informational purposes and must not, under any circumstances, be
taken as an independent clinical decision.

Inherent limitations of AI modules

• Data quality. AI results directly depend on the completeness and accuracy of the information
entered by the User; incorrect or incomplete complaints will lead to incorrect results;

• Hallucination. Generative models may create non-existent facts, sources, or recommendations
in a confident tone;

• Statistical bias. Disproportions in training data may result in decreased accuracy for certain
demographic groups;

• Lack of context. AI does not see, palpate, auscultate, or smell the patient — it cannot replace a
physical examination;

• Rare diseases. Model accuracy is significantly lower for rare pathologies;

• Time lag. Model knowledge is based on sources up to a certain date and may not reflect the
latest clinical protocols;

• Language and terminology. There may be differences in the interpretation of medical terms in
Uzbek, Russian, and English.

Legal status of AI results

A result generated by an AI module is not a diagnosis, not a medical conclusion, not an expert
opinion, and is not intended for use as medical evidence in judicial proceedings. AI results cannot
serve as a basis for resolving any insurance, labor, or social benefit issues.

Human-in-the-loop principle

Any decision that entails legal consequences for the User or significantly affects their health shall
be made only by a human — a licensed medical professional. The User has the right to object to a
decision based solely on automated processing and to request a human review (in accordance

**CHAPTER 7**

**LIMITATIONS OF TELEMEDICINE CONSULTATIONS**
Telemedicine is a supplementary form of providing medical care that does not replace a
face-to-face examination but rather complements it. During a remote consultation, the doctor
cannot physically examine the patient, perform palpation, percussion, or auscultation, which
objectively limits the accuracy of the diagnosis.

Situations NOT suitable for telemedicine

• Any emergency and life-threatening conditions;

• Open wounds, fractures, deep cuts, large-scale burns;

• Signs of severe respiratory or heart failure;

• Primary psychiatric diagnosis and severe psychotic states;

• Gynecological, urological, and proctological conditions requiring physical examination;

• Fever and weakness in newborns;

• Primary prescription of controlled substances (requiring a prescription form);

• Expert opinions for military-medical, driving, and weapon possession purposes;

• Unjustified remote issuance of certificates of temporary incapacity for work.

Technical quality and interruptions

The quality of video or audio communication depends on the User's internet speed, device,
browser, and operator network. Due to low communication quality, the doctor has the right to
terminate the consultation and recommend a face-to-face examination. The Operator is not
responsible for interruptions in third-party networks.

Consultation recording

A telemedicine session may be recorded as a medical document — the User shall be notified of
this in advance and their consent shall be obtained. The recording is stored for the period
specified in the Privacy Policy and is provided only on legal grounds.

Will my data be used for AI training?

Your personally identifiable medical records will not be used for model training without your

Can I take a medicine recommended by the AI?

No. The AI does not prescribe medication. Any drug shall be taken only upon a doctor's
prescription.

What should I do if I disagree with the AI result?

Use the "Object" button next to the result or write to info@med1.uz. The appeal will be reviewed
and the module will be corrected if necessary.

Can the AI identify an emergency situation?

The module may identify certain risk signs and urge you to contact 103. However, the absence of
such a warning does not mean the condition is safe.

Can I show the AI result to a doctor?

Yes, you can use it as a starting point for a conversation. However, the doctor is not obliged to
accept it as a medical document.
