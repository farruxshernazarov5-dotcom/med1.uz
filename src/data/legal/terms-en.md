### PLATFORM MODULES AND PROCEDURE FOR THEIR USE

4.1. Search and catalog module

4.1.1. The Platform enables searching for clinics, doctors, services, drug directories, and medical institutions. Search results are sorted by specialty, region, price, rating, working hours, distance, and other criteria.

4.1.2. Information in the catalog is provided by the Clinic/Doctor. The Operator is not responsible for its completeness or relevance; however, upon receiving a report of a clear error, it shall conduct an investigation within a reasonable timeframe.

4.1.3. Search results may contain advertising and paid placements (promotions); such positions shall be marked with "Advertising" or "Sponsored" labels.

4.2. Online Booking (Reservation) Module

4.2.1. The Patient selects an available time slot of the Doctor/Clinic and sends a booking request. The booking becomes effective after confirmation by the Clinic/Doctor.

4.2.2. A notification shall be sent to the Patient regarding the confirmation, modification, or cancellation of the booking.

4.2.3. The Patient may cancel the booking free of charge at least 3 (three) hours before the appointment, unless the Clinic has established different (more flexible) terms. Late cancellation or no-show may result in the loss of prepayment in accordance with the Clinic's rules.

4.2.4. The Clinic/Doctor may cancel a booking due to objective reasons (illness, emergency, schedule changes); in such cases, the prepayment shall be fully refunded or the appointment rescheduled.

4.2.5. The Operator is not responsible for the actual conduct of the appointment, its quality, or its duration.

4.3. Telemedicine (Online Consultation) Module

4.3.1. Telemedicine is a form of remote consultation and DOES NOT REPLACE an in-person examination, objective physical assessment, or instrumental diagnostics.

4.3.2. Before the start of a telemedicine session, the Patient shall provide a separate informed consent. Failure to provide consent shall be deemed a refusal of the session.

4.3.3. For technical disruptions that may occur during the consultation (internet speed, device malfunction), the Operator shall only be liable within the scope of its own server malfunctions.

4.3.4. Recording of the session shall be carried out only with the prior consent of both parties. Unauthorized distribution of the recording is considered a violation of the law and shall entail liability.

4.3.5. The following are PROHIBITED via telemedicine: management of emergency and resuscitation cases; prescription of substances restricted by law (narcotic, psychotropic); initial diagnosis of severe mental illnesses; issuance of expert opinions of legal significance.

4.3.6. If the Doctor determines that the condition cannot be resolved remotely, they must terminate the session and refer the Patient for an in-person consultation.

4.4. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Modules

4.4.1. The Platform may provide the following AI functions: preliminary triage by symptoms, structuring and summarizing medical documents, general information on interpreting analysis results, scribe assistant for doctors, preliminary marking of images, text translation, and chatbot assistance.

4.4.2. AI results are FOR INFORMATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY. They DO NOT CONSTITUTE and do not replace a medical diagnosis, prescription, clinical decision, or medical conclusion.

4.4.3. AI models may make errors, or provide incomplete or incorrect results ("hallucinations"). The User must verify any AI result with a qualified doctor.

4.4.4. Technologies from third-party providers may be used in AI modules. In such cases, the query text may be processed in a depersonalized form; the User consents to this.

4.4.5. It is PROHIBITED to enter excessive personally identifiable information (passport number, full address, bank card) into AI modules.

4.4.6. The Operator is not responsible for the consequences of independent medication intake, termination, or modification of treatment based on AI results.

4.4.7. The Operator has the right to use depersonalized and aggregated data to improve AI models. The User may opt-out of this through their personal account.

4.5. Electronic Medical Records (EMR) Module

4.5.1. The EMR module stores the Patient's medical history, diagnoses, prescriptions, analysis and examination results, images, and records regarding allergies and chronic diseases.

4.5.2. The Doctor/Clinic who entered the record is responsible for the medical content of EMR entries. The Operator is responsible for storage, encryption, backup, and access restriction.

4.5.3. The Patient has the right to access, copy, and export (PDF/JSON) their EMR records.

4.5.4. A Doctor shall have access to the Patient's records only based on the Patient's consent or within the scope of an active appointment/consultation. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

4.5.5. Each access is recorded in an audit log (who, when, which record was viewed). The Patient has the right to request the audit log.

4.5.6. Medical records are stored for the period established by law; even if the User deletes their account, records required by law may be kept in the archive.

4.6.1. An electronic prescription is generated only by an authorized Doctor, in the form and manner prescribed by law.

4.6.2. The Operator does not interfere with the content of the prescription and does not verify it from a medical perspective.

4.6.3. Data in the drug directory is based on open sources and manufacturer documentation and cannot serve as a basis for self-treatment.

4.6.4. The sale of prescription-only, narcotic, or psychotropic substances via the Platform is PROHIBITED.
