## CHAPTER 4

### PROCESSING PRINCIPLES

4.1. The Operator processes personal data in strict compliance with the following principles:

- Legality and fairness — data is processed only when there are grounds provided for by law;
- Purpose limitation — data is processed within the framework of specific, pre-declared purposes and is not used for purposes incompatible with them;
- Minimality — only the minimum amount of data necessary to achieve the purpose is collected;
- Accuracy — data is ensured to be correct, complete, and updated where necessary;
- Storage limitation — data is stored until the purpose is achieved or for the period established by law;
- Integrity and confidentiality — data is protected from unauthorized access, modification, and loss through technical and organizational measures;
- Transparency — the subject is informed about operations related to their data in an understandable form;
- Accountability — the Operator documents and is able to prove compliance with these principles. 4.2. The Operator applies privacy by design and privacy by default approaches starting from the design stage.

4.3. Before launching a new function or module, the Operator conducts a risk assessment and, where necessary, a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) procedure.
